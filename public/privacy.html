privacy

There are no accounts. Your set, the affirmations you write, and your settings are saved in this browser's storage on this device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Clearing this site's data in your browser erases your set. Another phone or tablet starts with its own set.

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics so we know whether families use the site. It sets no cookies and does not identify you; it records which page was visited, roughly where from, and how fast it loaded. Nothing you type is part of it.

The site is served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs (such as the address that asked for a page) for operating the service. There are no ads and no other trackers.
