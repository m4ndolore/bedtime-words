privacy

There are no accounts. Your set, the affirmations you write, and your settings are saved in this browser's storage on this device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Clearing this site's data in your browser erases your set. Another phone or tablet starts with its own set.

The site is served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs (such as the address that asked for a page) for operating the service. This site adds no analytics, ads or trackers.
